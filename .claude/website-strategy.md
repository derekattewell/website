# Personal Website Strategy Document

## Overview
A personal website that positions [Name] as a senior leader uniquely suited for this moment in the technology landscape—combining creative taste, technical depth, and hands-on execution across the full spectrum from strategy to implementation.

## Core Positioning
A senior leader who operates across the full stack—from high-level strategy down to the automation logic and the individual email template—with the rare combination of creative taste, quantitative rigor, and hands-on AI implementation experience.

### Key Differentiator
Taste. It's rare for someone with technical skills to have genuine creative sensibility, and rarer still for someone driving strategy to also make creative direction decisions. This is the "full package" positioning.

### Narrative Arc
Creative foundation (photography, design, branding from early age) → Quantitative training (physics undergrad, cosmology masters at McGill) → GTM experience (10 years in marketing and product development, full-funnel, every hat) → AI implementation (cutting edge, integrated into tech stacks and business processes, not just surface-level usage)

## Target Audience
- Recruiters seeking senior/executive GTM leadership
- Potential collaborators
- General professional presence

Primary conversion goal: Recruiter finds site → reaches out with senior opportunity

## Voice & Tone
- Confident but not boastful
- Down to earth, humble
- Plain language in opening sections, technical language acceptable in depth sections
- Editorial authority

## Messaging Framework

### Opening Statement (plain language, no jargon)

Working drafts:

> "Strategy is easy to talk about. Execution is where it falls apart. I work the full distance—from what we're trying to do, to the systems that do it, to the message that lands."

> "I help companies turn strategy into systems that actually execute—from the big picture down to the details that make it work. The vision, the tools, the automation, the creative. Most people specialize. I connect the layers."

Principle: Name the gap (strategy vs. execution), claim the span, imply depth without jargon.

### Why Now Section

AI is collapsing the distance between strategy and execution. The leaders who thrive will operate across that compressed space. This frames the diverse background as prescient, not scattered.

Territory for copy:
- The old divisions (strategist vs. executor, creative vs. technical, big picture vs. detail-oriented) are breaking down
- AI rewards those who can work across layers
- This moment requires a different kind of leader

### Background Blocks

Not a timeline or resume. Brief confident paragraphs for each area, framed as proof rather than biography:

**Creative Foundation**
Photography, design, branding from an early age. Establishes taste and visual sensibility.

**Quantitative Training**
Physics undergrad, cosmology masters at McGill. Establishes rigor, comfort with data, foundational understanding of quantitative disciplines.

**GTM Experience**
10 years in marketing and product development at a B2B research and consulting firm. Every hat worn. Full-funnel exposure. Understanding of how tools, data, automation, and AI shape what can be done at scale.

**AI Implementation**
Cutting edge, current. Not just using AI for writing emails or slides—integrated into tech stacks and business processes to improve both efficiency and quality of results.

### Principles / What I Believe (optional section)

Short, opinionated statements that give texture and personality:

- Details compound
- Automation without taste is just faster mediocrity
- Strategy that can't be implemented isn't strategy
- The best work happens when you don't have to hand off

### Contact CTA

Territory:

> "If you're building something ambitious and need someone who can operate at every level, let's talk."

Simple, confident, direct. Clean email link or form.

## Toggle / Mode Shift

The photography section is not separate from the professional story—it's evidence of the taste claim. But it deserves its own environment.

Working language options for the toggle/nav:
- Work / Eye
- The work / The eye
- Strategy / Vision
- Or simply a nav item: "Photography" without calling attention to the duality

The photography speaks for itself; we may not need to frame it as a statement.
